About Kishore Anekalla

I’m a PhD-trained bioinformatician who works on complex disease biology and the multimodal data behind it: genetics and GWAS, single-cell and bulk omics, clinical and real-world data, and knowledge graphs. My research background is in lung biology, aging and immunity, including fibrosis, macrophage biology and single-cell lung atlases. Today I build knowledge graphs and machine-learning pipelines for target discovery and translational insight, with graphs spanning cardiovascular, autoimmune and Parkinson’s disease. I also founded Arvy Technologies, a bioinformatics and AI consulting practice.
